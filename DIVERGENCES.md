# Divergence log

Every place the build differs from the handoff. **Status** is `Needs approval` (user-visible — copy, layout, a state, or a capability that doesn't work exactly as designed) or `Logged` (implementation-only).
Nothing marked *Needs approval* removes designed UI; these are additions for states the prototype didn't cover, or platform constraints. Review them and tell us which to keep, change or drop.
