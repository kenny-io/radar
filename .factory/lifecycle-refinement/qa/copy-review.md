# Independent copy review

Reviewed the frozen actual builders against the product UI, scheduling design, billing constants, authentication/token handlers, role permissions, backlink events and GSC readiness predicates. Applied the emails, copywriting and copy-editing skills: clarity, voice, benefit, support for claims, specificity, emotion and risk/friction. These are editorial checks, not word-match golden assertions.

The baseline is f616862c7dcefb1aabec9155972466f7b3aeaf36. `before-source/manifest.json` records raw source hashes; `before-previews/catalog.json` preserves 20 actual baseline lifecycle renderer variants. Legacy baseline source is preserved separately. Current render metadata and screenshots are in `renders/results.json`. The catalogue covers 28 families, including plaintext legacy notices; it does not count access-state variants as new families.

Course content retains two usable actions and a way to observe progress. Social instructions match Suggest ideas and editing/publishing controls; Backlinks includes joining when needed; AI wording does not guarantee recommendation or ranking. Prices, dates, recipients, URLs, tokens, business names and offering text are rendered/escaped by actual source builders, including bounded hostile inputs. Shared hidden preheaders were checked in HTML and real browser layout. All 28 families were exercised; five plaintext-only sender families were displayed in an explicitly labeled viewer, not represented as provider HTML.

Resolved findings: bounded long names caused mobile overflow (LC2-05-D1, independently reproduced and retested with identical inputs); the companion no-miss preheader promised a missed question (fixed and independently rerun). Final representative screenshots were visually inspected for trial, pretrial article workflow, Viewer invitation and no-miss report. No open material copy defect was observed. This does not establish inbox-client compatibility or email deliverability.
