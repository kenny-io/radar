Hi Alex,

You don’t have to start every conversation.

People are already asking questions, comparing tools and describing problems on Reddit, X, LinkedIn and Hacker News. Daily Brief finds relevant conversations and drafts replies in your voice. Your job is to decide where you have something useful to add.

  1. Open Daily Brief and review today’s conversations. Aim to find five you can help with; fewer thoughtful replies are better than forcing five. Read each original thread and edit the draft so it answers that person’s actual question.
  2. Copy a reply and post it in the original conversation, then mark it posted in Radar. Mention your product only when it helps answer the question, and be clear about your connection to it. Repeat this on days you have something useful to add.

Check progress: use Radar’s copied/posted states to track your follow-through. Revisit the original threads for responses. Copying a draft alone doesn’t mean it was posted or that anyone engaged.
