Radar creates social explainer videos from business content. Owners connect a YouTube channel, review the video and description, choose visibility and made-for-kids status, and upload now or schedule it. youtube.upload authorizes videos.insert for that approved upload; read-only scopes cannot create videos. youtube.readonly authorizes channels.list(mine=true) to identify and display the connected channel and videos.list(part=status,processingDetails) to report processing of Radar-created uploads and avoid duplicate retries. youtube.upload cannot authorize those read calls. We do not request broader youtube/youtube.force-ssl scopes or manage comments, subscriptions or existing videos. Tokens are encrypted server-side. Users can disconnect in Settings or revoke in Google Account permissions. Connected channel and upload-response data is used only for connection/publishing/status features, not sent to AI. Owner-selected source content drives video generation.
