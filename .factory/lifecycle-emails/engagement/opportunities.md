# Other useful follow-through opportunities

The social-pack reminder is included in this implementation: generated content with no copied/scheduled/posted action after24h. It shares social consent and the global optional-email cap with article-to-social prompts.

Two worthwhile future candidates, deliberately not added to the queue in this release:

- **Article awaiting editorial review:** a fully generated article remains in review for24–48h, with no approval/schedule/publish action. Link to that article, not a generic dashboard. Prioritize editorial review before an article-to-social prompt if both apply. Do not remind for published, queued, deleted or actively rewritten drafts. Would share a single work digest and explicit calendar/content consent.
- **Publishing needs attention:** a user-scheduled article or social post remains failed/held after a useful recovery window (for example, one hour), and no retry, cancellation or successful publication occurred. Explain the specific fix, such as reconnecting the channel; do not encourage more generation. These are delivery/recovery notices, requiring separate classification, suppression and supported-error mapping rather than treating them as marketing nudges.

Avoid login-frequency, streak, empty-dashboard and arbitrary inactivity campaigns. Their timing is less tied to a useful next action, and they risk competing with reminders about actual work. Do not promise35%growth; it is the owner's internal target, not a measured result or personalized forecast.
