Welcome

After account creation; continues from the Business Profile already created from the website.

Subject: Welcome to Radar
Reply to: support@thally.io

Founder note

One day after signup, unless optional setup emails are turned off.

Subject: Why we built Radar
Reply to: team@thally.io

Password reset

When someone requests a password reset for their password account.

Subject: Reset your Radar password
Reply to: support@thally.io

Trial started

When the subscription trial starts.

Subject: Your Radar trial is on
Reply to: support@thally.io

Trial ending

Before the active trial ends.

Subject: Your Radar trial ends soon
Reply to: support@thally.io

Payment receipt

When a paid subscription invoice is confirmed.

Subject: Your Radar receipt
Reply to: support@thally.io

Payment failed

When a subscription invoice payment fails.

Subject: Your Radar payment didn’t go through
Reply to: support@thally.io

Cancellation confirmed

When cancellation at the end of the current period is scheduled.

Subject: Your Radar cancellation is confirmed
Reply to: support@thally.io

Subscription ended

When the subscription ends.

Subject: Your Radar subscription has ended
Reply to: support@thally.io

Combined work reminders

Only while the expected next actions remain unfinished; at most one optional email per account per 24 hours.

Subject: A few things to pick up in Radar
Reply to: support@thally.io

Daily Brief follow-through

One hour after a successful run, only if no reply has been copied or posted.

Subject: Conversations worth joining
Reply to: support@thally.io

Calendar follow-through

24 hours after a generated plan, only if no topic has been approved or scheduled.

Subject: Choose the topics you want to publish
Reply to: support@thally.io

Article to social

Two hours after a generated article, only if social content has not been created from it.

Subject: Turn your article into social posts
Reply to: support@thally.io

Social follow-through

24 hours after social content is generated, only if none has been copied, scheduled or posted.

Subject: Your social posts are ready to schedule
Reply to: support@thally.io
