What happens before an article goes live

You don’t need to arrive with a month of article ideas or a spreadsheet of keywords.

Here’s the workflow once you start your Radar trial:

  • Radar uses your Business Profile and keyword research to propose topics in Content Calendar.
  • You approve the topics you want, then review and edit the articles Radar drafts.
  • Connect a supported publishing destination and schedule the articles you’re ready to put out.

Review comes first by default. Blog autopilot is a separate choice you can make later.

You can start with one article and decide how much of the workflow you want to automate.
