Hi Alex,

Before writing something new, it can be useful to look at what people already find on your site.

If your site has Google Search Console data, look for pages that get impressions but few clicks, or queries with an average position around 10–20.

Those are places to investigate. The page may need a clearer title, a fuller answer or an update to an older section.

During your Radar trial, connect Search Console and let the history sync. Performance shows the queries and pages alongside suggested changes you can review and add to Content Calendar.
