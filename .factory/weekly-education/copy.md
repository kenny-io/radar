# Five weekly lessons

Source of truth: `src/server/services/lifecycle/educationContent.ts`. Subjects personalize the primary business name, with a generic fallback; no website analysis or results are assumed. Rendered previews include active, pre-trial, lapsed and incomplete-setup examples. The welcome/founder emails remain separate; this course teaches the five features.

| Week | Feature | Example subject | Two actions | Check progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI Visibility | Does ChatGPT or Claude recommend Paper & Pixel? | Track two buyer questions; turn a missed question into a reviewed article topic | Compare Mentioned and Cited your site for stable questions |
| 2 | Daily Brief | Five conversations Paper & Pixel could join this week | Read and edit replies for useful conversations; post in context and mark posted | Review copied/posted states and responses in the original threads |
| 3 | Backlinks | Could another founder’s site introduce Paper & Pixel? | Join and set relevant topics; inspect and approve a useful proposal | Check Live links and verification status |
| 4 | Organic Search | What should Paper & Pixel publish next? | Review and schedule a focused topic; check the draft and publish | Follow calendar states and measured Search Console clicks/impressions |
| 5 | Social Marketing | Give Paper & Pixel’s next article another outing | Create a pack from an article or Business Profile; review and publish/schedule | Check publication status, then reactions/replies on the platform |

The requested five-reply idea is a goal, not a quota or claim that five suitable threads already exist. AI discovery copy does not claim older threads are preferred, promise recommendations or treat missing collections as zero. Backlink copy explains review, disclosure, qualifiers and actual placement. Organic Search distinguishes keyword estimates from measured performance. No35%gain claim. The course does not publish, join the network, schedule content or buy access on a recipient’s behalf.

Pre-trial/lapsed/setup variants explain the prerequisite and use the correct next-step CTA. There is no free-trial claim or invented customer result. The shared renderer supplies the existing crisp Radar logo, compact primary button, plaintext, first-party links and an education-only unsubscribe.

Rebuild previews from the repo root after runtime integration:

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/preview-weekly-education.mjs
```

Open `previews/index.html`; `previews/sequence.txt` contains all five active-workspace examples. Preview links/tokens are fictional and do not send, enroll or unsubscribe anyone.
